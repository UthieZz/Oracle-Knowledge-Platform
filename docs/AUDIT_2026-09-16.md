## Verdict

Dual runtime is the real architecture: locked `okc/` IR/compiler surface plus live `src/` + `studio/` product path.
Prior audits already fixed Flask launch, lazy Firestore registration, and post-compile provenance repair.
Remaining product failure modes are not compiler bugs:

1. Knowledge objects stay conversation-shaped. Quality is an analyzer problem. Do not fake it in Studio.
2. Provenance repair existed but publish defaulted to `strict=False`. Incomplete lineage could still reach Firestore.
3. Studio mapped missing `source_platform` to `Unmapped`, hiding the contract failure.
4. `__pycache__` is committed under `src/`. Dead weight. Do not treat as source.
5. Dockerfile exists. Architecture says do not expand Docker to run Studio. Leave as compiler placeholder.
6. Stage 4 local-first is decided, not implemented. Do not start a rewrite.
